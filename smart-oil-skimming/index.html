The Industrial Challenge

Floating oil is a quiet, expensive problem

Oil from industrial machines and processes can enter coolant and wastewater, forming a persistent surface layer that is costly to manage and easy to neglect.

◐

Oil Contamination

Floating oil builds up on the surface of coolant and wastewater over time.

≋

Water Quality

Contaminated coolant reduces fluid quality and can affect the surrounding environment.

✋

Manual Operation

Removing oil by hand is labour-intensive and inconsistent.

⚙

Maintenance

Oil and grime buildup increases wear and maintenance requirements.

◎

Monitoring

Without sensing, oil collection is inefficient and hard to track.

Our Solution

Smart Oil Skimming System

The system combines automated oil collection, oil-level sensing, intelligent motor-speed control, belt cleaning, and real-time monitoring in one continuous loop.

Every stage of the loop feeds the next — sensing informs control, control drives collection, and collection is reported back through the dashboard so the cycle keeps running without manual supervision.

How It Works

Eight steps, one continuous loop

01

Oil Detection

The oil-level sensor detects the oil level and thickness on the coolant surface.

02

Sensor Signal

The sensor sends the detected information to the controller.

03

Motor Speed Control

The controller adjusts motor speed based on the incoming oil-level information.

04

Conveyor Collection

The conveyor belt moves through contaminated coolant/water and collects floating oil.

05

Oil Scraping

A scraper removes the accumulated oil from the belt.

06

Oil Transfer

Separated oil is transferred into an oil collection container.

07

Belt Cleaning

A cleaning mechanism cleans the conveyor belt for the next cycle.

08

Continuous Operation

The system repeats the detection → control → collection process automatically.

System Architecture

From sensing to the dashboard

Hover any block to see what it does.

CONTAMINATED COOLANT
Coolant carrying a floating oil layer enters the skimmer's working area.
OIL LEVEL SENSOR
Detects the thickness/level of the floating oil layer.
ESP32 CONTROLLER
Reads the sensor and decides how the motor should respond.
DATA PROCESSING
Converts raw sensor input into a motor-speed decision.
MOTOR DRIVER
Translates the controller's command into motor power.
DC MOTOR
Drives the conveyor belt at the commanded speed.
CONVEYOR BELT
Physically carries collected oil upward out of the coolant.
SCRAPER
Removes oil from the belt surface at the top of its travel.
OIL COLLECTION CONTAINER
Holds the separated oil for later removal/reuse.
CLEANED COOLANT
Coolant with reduced floating oil, ready for continued use.

IoT Connection
